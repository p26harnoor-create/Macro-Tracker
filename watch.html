§5.1

Calendar

#
  • 7 Oct · 10:00 ISTRBI MPC decision. Consensus +25 bp to 5.50%. Watch stance wording and the updated Q3/Q4 FY27 CPI path. → §2.1
  • ~10 OctIndia IIP (Aug); US CPI (Sept) — mid-month, date per BLS calendar.
  • 12 OctIndia CPI (Sept). Food composition, core/services, rural vs urban. → §2.2
  • 14 OctIndia WPI (Sept). Does the CPI–WPI gap close from above or below?
  • mid-Oct →Q2 FY27 earnings: margin compression from oil/metal inputs is the WPI→earnings test. → §2.7
  • late OctUS hyperscaler results — AI capex guidance. → §3.7
  • 27–28 OctFOMC. Hold vs second hike; dots. → §3.1
  • early NovUS Treasury quarterly refunding — coupon sizes, buybacks. The term-premium event. → §3.2
  • early NovUS payrolls (Oct).
  • DecRBI MPC — Goldman's second hike pencilled in. FOMC 15–16 Dec (per usual schedule — verify).

§5.5

Source hierarchy

#

Never cite Tier 3 when Tier 1 exists for the same factual claim.

Tier 1 — Primary

  • RBI, MoSPI, Ministry of Finance / CGA, PIB, DPIIT, CCIL, NSE/BSE, NSDL, AMFI
  • Fed, BLS, BEA, US Treasury, CBO, EIA
  • IMF, World Bank, IEA, OECD

Tier 2 — High-quality reporting

  • Reuters, Bloomberg, FT, WSJ
  • Business Standard, Economic Times, Mint (selectively)

Tier 3 — Commentary

  • Broker research, research houses, analyst blogs
  • Use for interpretation, not for facts
