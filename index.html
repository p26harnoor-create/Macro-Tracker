Five things that matter this week

  1. The RBI hiked — and the market looked past it +25 bp to 5.50% on 7 Oct, unanimous, stance to "calibrated tightening", FY27 CPI raised to 5.2% (Q3 at 6.0%), GDP to 7.1%. "Hike or pause" is the guidance; December is live. §2.1
  2. 8 October: every leg of the bear case fired at once Brent +5% to $105.30 on US–Iran escalation talk, US 10Y 5.35% (highest since 2002), USD/INR 97.13, FPIs ₹31,282 cr out in eight sessions, Nifty −1.64% to 22,231, India VIX +10%. Rupee oil price through ₹10,000/bbl. §2.4 · §2.7
  3. The Fed is not the relief valve FOMC minutes and Waller point to further hikes; December ~71% priced. The long end rose with hike odds — the asymmetry that keeps the dollar bid. §3.1
  4. CPI 12 Oct, WPI 14 Oct RBI's Q2 path implies ~4.9%; a print above 5% with core rising validates a December hike. Watch the CPI–WPI gap. §2.2
  5. Two structural stories filled in India private capex at a record ₹3.2 trn for FY27 but announcements −56% in March; US hyperscaler capex $720–745bn for 2026 and rising into 2027. §2.8 · §3.7
